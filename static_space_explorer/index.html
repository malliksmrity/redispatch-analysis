Redispatch explorer: cost, volume and who orders it

Pick a measure and a period. Monthly values come from SMARD (July 2022 to April 2026). The operator chart uses the Netztransparenz list of individual measures, which starts on 31 December 2023. Only the months that also exist in the monthly series (to April 2026) are counted here, so the totals are a little lower than on the project page, which counts all measures up to 8 September 2026.






Data: SMARD, Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, CC BY 4.0, and Netztransparenz (the four transmission operators). See the project page for the trend tests (cost per MWh has clearly fallen, volume has not changed clearly).
