GridSimEnv: resolve a grid congestion at minimum cost

In this hour the market schedule sends more power from the windy north to the demand in the south than the line can carry. Cut the flow by redispatch: reduce generation in the north, raise it by the same amount in the south. Find the cheapest plan, then watch the 3D grid, the pylons and the charts react.

What is real and what is assumed: the hourly German wind series is real (SMARD, 12,961 hours). The two-zone grid, the share of wind sent north to south (20 %), the line limit (72 % of the scheduled flow), the plant limits and all prices are stylised assumptions. The 3D plants and pylons are illustrative. The problem has a provable optimum, so every plan can be graded.
