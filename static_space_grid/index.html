GridSimEnv: resolve a grid congestion at minimum cost

In this hour the market schedule sends more power from the north to the south than the line can carry. You must cut the north-south flow by redispatch: reduce generation in the north and increase it by the same amount in the south. Pick the cheapest plan.

What is real and what is assumed: the hourly German wind series is real (SMARD, 12,961 hours). The two-zone grid, the share of wind sent north to south (20 %), the line limit (72 % of the scheduled flow) and all plant limits and prices are stylised assumptions. The problem has a provable optimum, so every plan can be graded.
